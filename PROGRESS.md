## Next steps (start of M6)
1. Slow-mo polish (already: final kill + multi-kill slow-mo, Bullet Time) — add a killcam zoom for the final kill.
2. Instant replay: record intents per tick (`packIntent`) + seed → re-simulate the last ~6 s of a round and
   play it back at the round end (the sim is deterministic per seed; tests already prove it).
3. Ghost mode: dead players float as ghosts that can nudge props/items a little (Intent-driven ghost body).
4. Chaos modifiers (lobby toggles): big heads, low gravity, explosive everything, one-hit kills, melee only,
   fast weapons, slippery floors — data table applied to WorldSettings/fighter.
5. Bounty crown: the leader wears a crown; killing them is worth +1 (Deathmatch) / announced (Brawl).
6. Post-match awards screen from `match.stats` (most kills, most damage, most suicides, "pacifist"...).
7. Fighter creator: edit appearance parts in the lobby (cycle hair/face/hat/top/colors), saved profiles.
